Small Claims Dispute Guide

How arbiters and judges evaluate rental wear & tear.

1. The 72-Hour Legal Window:

Under standard lease terms, flaws not recorded in writing during initial move-in are legally presumed to have occurred during your tenancy.

2. Ordinary Wear vs Tenant Damage:

Landlords are prohibited from charging tenants for paint fading, light carpet wear, or pre-existing fixture oxidation. Your LandlordProof dossier proves Day 1 status.

3. Statutory Penalties:

If the landlord withholds your deposit past the statutory deadline (e.g. 21 days in CA, 30 days in TX), most judges award double or triple damages.
