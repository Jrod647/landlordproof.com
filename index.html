Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about LandlordProof or security deposit rights?

How does LandlordProof protect my security deposit?
LandlordProof creates an indisputable photographic and statutory paper trail of your apartment's condition on Day 1. By stamping exact GPS coordinates, UTC timestamps, and cryptographic tamper seals into photo pixels, landlords cannot falsely claim that pre-existing damage was caused by you during your tenancy.
Why can't I just take regular photos with my phone camera?
Standard smartphone camera photos only store metadata (EXIF data) that can be easily edited or stripped when uploaded or messaged. LandlordProof burns indelible, visible watermarks directly into the photo raster along with a courtroom-formatted inspection dossier and state statutory citations that judges and small-claims arbiters take seriously.
Is LandlordProof really 100% free?
Yes! LandlordProof is completely free with no subscriptions, paywalls, or in-app purchases. All features—including timestamp watermarks, room defect checklists, 50-state legal statutes, and courtroom PDF dossier generation—are accessible for free, supported by non-intrusive sponsor ads.
Are my walkthrough photos uploaded to any servers?
No! LandlordProof is built on a 100% offline-first, local privacy architecture. All property photos, notes, and deposit amounts remain securely on your phone. Nothing is ever sent to external cloud servers, advertisers, or trackers.
What happens if my landlord fails to return my deposit on time?
LandlordProof tracks your state's statutory return deadline (e.g. 14 days in SD/VT, 21 days in CA, 30 days in TX/FL). If the deadline passes without your deposit or an itemized deduction accounting, LandlordProof automatically drafts a formal Statutory Demand Letter citing exact state code and statutory penalties (up to 2x or 3x treble damages in court).
