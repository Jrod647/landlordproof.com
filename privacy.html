Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Zero-Tracking Commitment

LandlordProof ("we", "our", or "the App") is built from the ground up on an offline-first, privacy-by-design architecture. We believe your rental property records, damage photos, lease documents, and financial deposit amounts are strictly personal. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transmit your personal data to any external advertising networks, trackers, or data brokers.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

When you use LandlordProof, all information you input—including property addresses, tenant names, lease dates, security deposit amounts, damage descriptions, photos, and GPS coordinates—is stored solely on your local device storage.

  • No Account Required: You can use LandlordProof without creating an account or providing an email address to use the core app.
  • No Cloud Photo Uploads: Your walkthrough photos and evidence records remain strictly on your phone unless you choose to export or share a PDF report yourself.

3. Camera, Photo Library & Location Permissions

  • Camera: Used exclusively when you take evidence photos of property conditions and pre-existing flaws.
  • Photo Library: Used when you import existing photos of damage or comparison shots into your inspection reports, or save generated reports.
  • Location (GPS): Used solely on-device at the moment you take or stamp a photo to verify the geographic coordinates and reverse-geocode the address for legal evidence watermarks. GPS coordinates are burned directly into image pixels on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.

4. 100% Free App & Ad-Supported Privacy (Google AdMob)

LandlordProof contains zero in-app purchases and zero paid subscriptions. We do not collect or store credit card, banking, or billing information.

The App displays non-intrusive advertisements served via Google AdMob to support continuous maintenance and service delivery. Google AdMob may collect and process non-sensitive device identifiers, IP addresses, and diagnostic telemetry for ad serving and fraud prevention in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Your rental property records, damage photographs, notes, and deposit amounts remain strictly stored on your local device and are never shared with or transmitted to Google AdMob or any third party.

5. Apple & Google Compliance (Privacy Manifest)

LandlordProof complies with Apple's Privacy Manifest requirements (declaration NSPrivacyTracking: false) and Google Play Data Safety standards, ensuring complete transparency and zero unauthorized third-party telemetry tracking.
